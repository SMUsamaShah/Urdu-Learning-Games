# RV AppStudios — *ABC Spelling*, for reference

A second app from the same studio as `../rv-appstudios/`, this one about
building words rather than learning letters. Kept for the same reason: this is
where the spelling activities in this app are going, and a reference nobody can
find is one that stops being used.

## The thing worth copying

**The same game has two difficulty modes, and they differ by one letter being
visible.** In `spell-cvc-hint.jpg` the last slot of `C A _` is drawn with a
faint T already in it, so the child is matching a shape. In
`spell-cvc-no-hint.jpg` the last slot of `B A _` is empty white, so they have to
know it. Everything else about the screen is identical — same board, same tray,
same tile.

That is a cheap and honest difficulty curve: no new content, no new screen, and
the step between the two is exactly the step from recognition to recall.

**The rest of the layout, which is consistent across both:**

- A picture of the word at the top of the board, and nothing else — no written
  word to copy from.
- The word being built as **jigsaw tiles that interlock**, each in its own
  colour, filled left to right. A filled tile keeps its colour; an empty slot is
  plain white.
- One tray under the board holding the letters still to place. In these two
  shots it holds exactly one tile, so the first rounds cannot be got wrong by
  accident.
- The character stands at the left doing nothing in particular, and reacts.
- A progress bar of four notches at the top left, ending in a trumpet.
- No text instructions anywhere on the play screen.

## The activities they ship

Worth having written down, because it is a list of ideas rather than a list of
screenshots. Not all of them transfer — Urdu has no CVC pattern and no silent
letters — but most do.

**Spellings**: Spell It (picture, tap letters into slots) · Fill in the Blank
(one letter missing from the middle) · Blank Spelling (every slot empty) ·
Rhyming Words (pick the one that rhymes) · Word Connect (letters in a grid) ·
Word Sentences (build a sentence from word tiles) · Fishing · Egg & Dino ·
Flash Cards.

**CVC Words**, with a Vowels toggle in the corner: Spell CVC · Build Word ·
Missing Vowel (the vowel row is always A E I O U) · Balloon Pop · Memory Match ·
CVCC Words · Crane.

The shape of that list is the point: **one mechanic, many wrappers.** Spell CVC,
Build Word, Crane and CVCC Words are the same act of putting letters in order,
dressed as a board, a hand, a crane and a train. That is how they get seven
activities out of one idea, and why a child does not tire of it.

**And the menus:** a grid of large square cards, each with a *picture of the
activity being played* and its name above. Nine of them fit on one screen at
phone size, which is more than this app's panel of extra games manages, and
they are readable because each picture is a miniature of the actual board.
